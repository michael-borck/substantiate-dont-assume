# Substantiate, Don't Assume: current editorial blueprint

Updated 15 September 2026 after author approval of the developmental revision.
This replaces the draft blueprint; git history retains that version.

## Promise and audience

Teach a first bounded information-security readiness assessment. Readers bring basic knowledge of accounts, permissions, logs, backups and cloud responsibilities. They learn to select criteria, test supplied records, write findings, track retests and defend a conclusion. The book is not a standards manual or certification course.

## Structure retained

Twelve core chapter filenames and titles remain stable.

| Chapter | Main job |
|---|---|
| 1 Why | Mandate, ethics, independence and evidence basics |
| 2 Risk | Explicit rating assumptions and risk-based test depth |
| 3 Which | Framework purpose, SoA/applicability and supplier reliance |
| 4 Scope | Scope versus coverage/depth; a small executable procedure |
| 5 Expect | Bounded technical objectives and a concern cleared by evidence |
| 6 Assess | Limited design assessment and an operating-evidence agenda |
| 7 Prove | Evidence quality, numerical sampling, census, custody and interviewing |
| 8 Comply | Both policy deadlines and distinct review populations |
| 9 Protect | Privacy flow, legal boundaries and complete finding subissues |
| 10 Recover | Tested service targets, dependencies and response safety |
| 11 Report | Corrected 5C+R template, causes, rates and closure ledger |
| 12 Conclude | Complete bounded opinion, board defence and independent transfer |

Act I produces mandate, criteria and a test programme. Act II produces working papers, a finding ledger and an opinion. Evidence is staged for learning; real engagements may overlap interviews and document/operating review.

## Canonical case and resources

SDA-2026.1 is a book-only Tessera scenario with original synthetic inputs and a 30 June 2026 evidence cutoff. It is not a mirror of the live companion site. The complete 48-row population is printed as well as downloadable. Missing retest evidence remains missing; it is not silently replaced by successful remediation.

The appendices provide the method, evidence pack, answer checks, transfer rubric, primary-source guide and interview practice. The live Tessera site remains optional and separately versioned. No core task needs an AI account, partner or external dataset.

## Voice and practice

Keep chapter titles, navigation markers and Evidence Locker milestones. Each chapter has a timed solo task. Chapters 3, 7 and 12 alone retain extended optional AI endings. Field Notes are bounded illustrative drafts; do not present them as measured model transcripts.

The author approved fictional framing of unattributed audit anecdotes. Preserve verified biographical attribution; invent no personal experience. House style prohibits prose em dashes and repetitive thesis payoffs. Navigation emoji remain intentional.

## Reporting and accuracy

The author-created 5C+R template uses Criteria, Condition, Cause, Consequence, Correction, plus Recommendation/response covering cause-directed corrective action. It is not a universal certification rubric.

F01–F05 and bounded pass P01 must reach the final opinion. The case supports deferring Stage 2; it does not establish whole-ISMS Stage 1 readiness or award certification. Legal interpretation and exact normative text require appropriate authorised review.
